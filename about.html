About Us

A Family Helping Families

Brown Financial Group is a growing father and son independent life insurance brokerage built on one straightforward idea: families deserve honest guidance when it comes to protecting what matters most.

Our Mission

Making Coverage Work for Real Families

Most people know they need life insurance, but very few understand how it actually works, what they qualify for, or how to compare their options. That is the gap we exist to fill.

John Brown has spent the past several years helping families across the country navigate their coverage options as a licensed life insurance agent. Josh Brown brings the operational and technical side, building the systems that keep the agency running efficiently and making sure every client interaction is organized, responsive, and personal. As the team grows, that foundation becomes more important. Together with our agents, we handle everything from the first conversation to the policy in hand.

How We Work

What Sets Us Apart

No Carrier Bias
We work with dozens of carriers and have no obligation to any of them. That independence means we can always recommend the plan that actually fits — not the one with the best commission.
People Over Policies
We don't chase volume. We take the time to understand your situation — your mortgage, your income, your family — and build a recommendation around that, not the other way around.
Transparent Pricing
Life insurance doesn't have to be a mystery. We walk you through what you're paying, what you're getting, and why — so you can make an informed decision with full confidence.
Our Network

The Symmetry Financial Group Advantage

We are contracted through Symmetry Financial Group, one of the largest independent marketing organizations in the country. That relationship gives us access to a wide range of top-rated carriers including Mutual of Omaha, Foresters Financial, Americo, and others, so we can shop the market on your behalf rather than being tied to a single company's products. Our job is to find what is right for you, not what is easiest for us.

50+ Carrier Relationships

Through Symmetry's network, we have contracted access to over 50 top-rated carriers — something an independent agent starting from scratch would take years to build on their own.

No Captive Restrictions

Unlike captive agents who can only sell one company's products, we are never locked into a single carrier. If a better plan exists for your family, we can find it and offer it.

Licensed Nationwide

Symmetry's infrastructure supports licensure across the country. Whether you're in California, Texas, or Florida, we can serve your family without you having to start over with someone new.

Underwriting Support

Symmetry's back-office resources and carrier relationships give us access to underwriting guidance that helps us find coverage options even for clients with complex health histories.

A captive agent can only offer you what their one company sells. We shop the entire market on your behalf — across dozens of carriers — and only recommend what genuinely makes sense for your situation.
